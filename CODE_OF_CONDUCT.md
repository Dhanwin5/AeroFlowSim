# Code of conduct

AeroFlowSim welcomes everyone: students, CubeSat teams, researchers and engineers, whatever their experience,
background or identity.

**Expected:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give and accept constructive criticism of work (not of
people), credit others' contributions, and keep discussions on topic.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, publishing others'
private information, and any behaviour that would be unwelcome in a professional setting.

**Enforcement:** the maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may
ban people who repeatedly do so. Report problems privately to the maintainer (contact as in SECURITY.md).

This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (contributor-covenant.org).
